## Becoming a Maintainer

If you have made significant contributions and are interested in becoming a
maintainer, please reach out to any current maintainer or open an issue.

Maintainers are expected to:
- Review pull requests in their area in a timely manner
- Help triage and respond to issues
- Participate in design discussions
- Uphold the code of conduct and contribution guidelines
